Freedom at Last

You stumble through the laundry exit and burst into the hospital's loading yard. The door slams behind you, its rusted hinges groaning as you struggle to catch your breath. Rain pounds against the cracked pavement, filling the potholes with dark water and washing old stains toward the storm drains. Rows of abandoned delivery carts sit beneath a sagging metal awning, and torn plastic sheets flap violently against a chain-link fence. A delivery truck rests near the loading dock with its rear doors hanging open, its interior completely dark. You force yourself to keep moving. Every breath burns in your lungs, and your legs feel unsteady after the desperate escape through the hospital. Behind you, something strikes the laundry door hard enough to shake its frame. You hear the doctor's voice through the metal. "You have no idea what you've done!" The words are followed by a prolonged scraping sound, as if he is dragging something heavy across the floor. You refuse to look back. Ahead, the loading gate stands partially open, its damaged motor struggling to move the heavy metal panels. Beyond it lies a narrow service road bordered by overgrown trees and abandoned industrial buildings. A distant siren rises above the sound of the rain, followed by the faint flash of red and blue lights somewhere beyond the trees. You rush toward the gate, but a sudden movement near the truck catches your attention. For an instant, you see a figure standing behind the open rear doors. It wears a hospital gown identical to yours. When lightning flashes across the yard, the figure is gone. You reach the gate and squeeze through the opening just as the laundry door bursts open behind you. The doctor steps into the rain, his white coat whipping in the wind. He does not run after you. Instead, he stands beneath the awning and watches as you stagger toward the road. Even from a distance, you can hear his final words. "I'll see you again." You keep running until the hospital disappears behind the darkness and the trees. Only when the sirens grow louder do you finally stop. You have escaped Blackwood Hospital, but the memory of the doctor's voice follows you into the night. As you look down at your trembling hands, you notice a faint red mark around one wrist, shaped exactly like the restraints you escaped.

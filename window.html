The Broken Window

You climb the remaining steps and reach the broken window just as the doctor's shadow appears on the landing below. Cold air rushes through the jagged opening, carrying the smell of rain, wet leaves, and distant traffic. You push aside the remaining shards of glass and look outside. The hospital's exterior wall is stained with years of rainwater, and the courtyard below is filled with weeds, overturned benches, and abandoned medical carts. A security lamp flickers near the perimeter fence, briefly illuminating the hospital's cracked stone facade. Directly beneath the window, a sloping maintenance roof extends toward the courtyard. Its surface is slick with rain, and several sections of roofing material have peeled away, exposing dark wooden beams underneath. Beyond the roof, you can see a narrow ledge that might allow you to reach a lower section of the building. It is not a safe descent, but staying inside the stairwell is becoming impossible. Behind you, the doctor's shoes scrape against the landing. He pauses when he sees the open window. For several seconds, neither of you moves. Then he steps into view, his white coat stained and hanging loosely from his shoulders. His face remains hidden behind the shadows, but you can see the outline of a surgical instrument dangling from one hand. "You really think the outside will save you?" he asks calmly. "Everyone who leaves this place eventually finds their way back." He takes another step toward you. You swing one leg over the windowsill and feel the cold rain soak through your clothes. The roof shifts slightly beneath your weight, and loose gravel slides toward the courtyard below. You notice a metal ladder attached to the wall a few feet away, but its lower section has broken loose.

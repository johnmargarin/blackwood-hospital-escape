The Stairwell

You throw yourself through the stairwell door and pull it shut just as the doctor's shadow appears on the other side. The heavy door slams into its frame, sending a sharp metallic echo through the concrete shaft. You begin descending the stairs two at a time, gripping the cold railing as your shoes slip on the damp steps. The stairwell smells of wet cement and rust, and somewhere in the darkness below, water drips at irregular intervals. Halfway down, you reach a landing marked with a faded sign reading BASEMENT. The door beneath it has been secured with a thick chain and an oversized padlock. Several deep scratches mark the metal around the lock, and a dark liquid has dried beneath the handle. From somewhere on the other side comes a slow, rhythmic tapping. Three knocks, a pause, and then three more. You lean closer, hoping to hear something that might explain what is down there, but the tapping suddenly stops. A voice whispers through the narrow gap beneath the door. "Don't let him bring you down here." Before you can react, a loud crash erupts above you. The doctor has entered the stairwell. His shoes scrape against the concrete as he begins making his way down, humming the same lullaby you heard when you woke up. The sound grows louder with every step, occasionally interrupted by the click of his keys against the railing. You look for another way out. One floor above, a broken window opens onto the exterior of the hospital, although the jagged glass and steep drop make the route dangerous. Below, a heavy fire door leads toward the laundry and loading areas. Its push bar is covered in grime, but it appears undamaged. The chained basement door remains behind you, and the tapping has started again, faster this time.

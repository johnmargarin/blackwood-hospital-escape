The Dark Corridor

You slip into the main corridor, pulling the door shut behind you as quietly as possible. The hallway stretches farther than you expected, lined with abandoned patient rooms and shattered observation windows. Dust coats the floor, disturbed only by a series of muddy footprints that lead toward the stairwell. The air reeks of disinfectant, mold, and something metallic that you try not to think about. Overhead, a security light swings from a damaged ceiling fixture, casting long, distorted shadows across the walls. At the far end of the corridor, a red EXIT sign glows above a heavy metal stairwell door. It seems like the safest choice, but you notice fresh scratches around the handle, as if someone has recently tried to force it open. Beside you, the elevator doors stand open. A weak emergency light illuminates the cramped interior, revealing a control panel with several missing buttons and a dark stain across the floor. The elevator cable creaks somewhere overhead, followed by a deep metallic groan that echoes through the shaft. Behind you, the doctor's footsteps return. They are slow and deliberate, as though he knows you are nearby and is enjoying the hunt. You hear him stop outside your former room. A moment later, he laughs softly. "Now, now. You shouldn't be wandering around without permission." The stairwell might offer a direct route toward the lower floors, but the elevator could take you somewhere the doctor would never expect. The footsteps are getting closer, and the security light suddenly goes dark.

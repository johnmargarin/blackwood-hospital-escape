The Elevator

The elevator shudders violently as it descends, its rusted cables groaning somewhere above the ceiling. Each passing floor is marked by a flickering red number on the control panel, but the numbers become increasingly erratic the deeper you travel. You press the button for the ground floor, hoping to return to a familiar part of the hospital, but the panel responds with a sharp electrical crackle. The button goes dark, and the elevator continues downward. A cold draft slips through the gap between the doors, carrying the unmistakable smell of damp concrete, old paper, and something vaguely chemical. You grip the railing as the elevator suddenly slows. The lights overhead flicker twice before shutting off completely. For several seconds, you are trapped in total darkness, listening to the mechanical groaning of the elevator as it struggles to stop. Then the emergency light above the control panel flickers to life, bathing the cramped interior in a weak red glow. The elevator comes to a halt with a heavy metallic bang. The doors slide open.

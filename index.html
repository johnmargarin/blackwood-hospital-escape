The Abandoned Hospital

You slowly regain consciousness to the sound of a fluorescent light buzzing above your head. Your skull throbs, your mouth is dry, and a bitter chemical smell fills your nose. When you attempt to sit up, you discover that you are lying on a narrow hospital bed surrounded by cracked white tiles and rusted medical equipment. Leather restraints hang loosely from your wrists, their buckles broken as if someone had tried to secure you in a hurry. You have no memory of how you arrived here, and the last thing you remember is a pair of headlights cutting through the darkness. A soft humming drifts through the corridor outside your room. It is an old lullaby, slow and almost gentle, but something about the tune makes your stomach tighten. The humming stops abruptly, followed by the squeak of shoes against the floor. A shadow stretches beneath the door, pauses, and slowly moves away. You hear the metallic rattle of keys and the quiet voice of a man muttering something about a patient who should never have awakened. Your heart begins to pound. You have only seconds before whoever is outside discovers that your bed is empty. To your left, a narrow corridor disappears into darkness, with a flickering green EXIT sign at its far end. To your right, a supply room door hangs slightly open, revealing shelves of medical supplies and a possible hiding place. Straight ahead, the main hallway leads toward the elevators and stairwell.

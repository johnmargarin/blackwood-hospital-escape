The Boiler Room

You reach the service tunnel by following a narrow passage behind the boiler room. The air grows hotter and heavier as you descend, and the walls become slick with condensation. Ancient pipes run along the ceiling, releasing intermittent bursts of steam that hiss into the darkness. Each step sends an echo through the confined passage, making it difficult to tell whether the sounds behind you belong to your own footsteps or someone following close behind. The tunnel is older than the rest of the hospital. Its brick walls are stained black in places, and faded warning signs hang crookedly from rusted brackets. Several sections of flooring have collapsed, exposing drainage channels filled with dark water. You pass a row of abandoned carts and a collection of sealed metal containers marked with faded medical hazard symbols. One container has been forced open from the inside, its lid bent outward around a series of deep scratches. At the far end of the passage, you discover a heavy door marked SERVICE ACCESS. Beside it is an electrical control box mounted to the wall. Its cover hangs open, revealing a complicated arrangement of wires, switches, and corroded components. A faded label identifies the system as the hospital's emergency lock control. If you can disable the system, you might be able to unlock the laundry exit and reach the loading yard. You move closer, but the sound of footsteps interrupts you. A beam of flashlight sweeps across the opposite wall. You immediately duck behind a pipe as the light moves toward the control box. The doctor enters the tunnel slowly, examining the floor and running his fingers along the damp brickwork. He appears to know exactly where you are going. "You've always been curious," he says. "That was your problem the first time, too." The words send a chill through you. The first time? You glance at the control box. Several switches are labeled, but most of the writing has faded. A warning sticker indicates that cutting power incorrectly could seal the lower doors permanently. Near the bottom, you spot a red emergency lever protected by a cracked plastic cover. Beside it, a small indicator light flashes in time with the buzzing overhead lamps. The flashlight moves closer. You could attempt to disable the locks, risking a power surge that might trap you inside the tunnel. Alternatively, you could slip through the service door and continue toward the loading yard, leaving the locks untouched. The steam pressure begins to rise, filling the tunnel with a low, vibrating rumble. Then the doctor turns off his flashlight. In the sudden darkness, you hear him whisper directly behind you. "Found you.
